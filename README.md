# Python-Zomato-EDA-Project
Analyze the Zomato restaurant dataset to understand customer preferences and business trends
Identify factors influencing restaurant ratings, votes, and popularity
Explore cost distribution and its relationship with ratings

## Dataset Description
Contains ~50,000 restaurant records from Zomato platform
Columns include: name, location, cuisines, cost, rating, votes, restaurant type, etc.
Data covers multiple cities and restaurant categories

## Goals
Perform step‑wise Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Detect missing values and clean dataset for consistency
Visualize top cuisines, restaurant types, and city‑wise distribution
Generate insights useful for business decision making and customer engagement

## Observations
Many restaurants have zero votes, showing low customer engagement
Most restaurants fall in low to medium cost range (below ₹1000), making them affordable
Very few restaurants are expensive (above ₹2000)
Quick Bites and Casual Dining are the most common restaurant types
North Indian cuisine dominates overall, followed by combinations like North Indian + Chinese and South Indian
BTM has the highest number of restaurants, followed by HSR and Koramangala 5th Block, while Bellandur has the lowest among the top 10

## Final Insights
Majority of restaurants are affordable (cost for two usually below ₹2000)
Quick Bites and Casual Dining dominate the market
North Indian cuisine leads overall demand
